# Abuse policy

beamup is a shared, free service. To keep it working for everyone, addons deployed to it must not:

1. **Scan or probe other servers.** No finding, testing or harvesting open proxies, no port scanning, and no connecting to large numbers of hosts you don't run. Fetching content from the sites your addon is built for is fine.
2. **Download or store media.** Don't keep video files on beamup, and keep your addon's disk use under 5 GB.
3. **Redeploy excessively.** Up to 24 deploys per addon per day.
4. **Use several accounts to get around these rules.**

We may suspend any addon that harms the service or other users, without notice. Repeated or deliberate violations get the account banned.
